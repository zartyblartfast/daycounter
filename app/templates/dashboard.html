Getting Started — Workflow
  1. Settings — Set your departure date and first non-resident tax year. This configures your midnight targets and limits.
  2. Travels — Log each trip (past or future). Mark UK stays so the system can count midnights spent in the UK.
  3. Evidence Vault — Upload boarding passes, receipts, and other documents to support your residency records.
  4. Dashboard (here) — Automatically shows your progress: midnights used, risk status, and the 5-year TNR clock.
  5. Heatmap — Visual calendar of your UK presence, auto-generated from your travel records.

Tip: Start with Settings, then add your travel records. Everything else updates automatically.
