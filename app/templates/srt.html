About the Statutory Residency Test

The Statutory Residency Test (SRT) determines your UK tax residency status based on a series of tests examining your ties to the UK, days spent in the country, and your work patterns. It was introduced in April 2013 and applies to all individuals.

The SRT consists of three main parts:

  • Automatic Overseas Test — If you meet any of these conditions, you are automatically non-resident.
  • Automatic UK Test — If you meet any of these conditions, you are automatically UK resident.
  • Sufficient Ties Test — If neither automatic test is met, your residency depends on the number of ties you have to the UK combined with the number of days spent here.

Future enhancements will include an interactive SRT flowchart and tie assessment tool to help you determine your residency status step by step.

How Profiles Work

Each profile corresponds to a tax year relative to your departure. The app automatically selects the correct profile based on the First Full Non-Resident Tax Year set in Settings.

Profile Mapping
  • Year 1: First full non-resident tax year
  • Year 2: Second full non-resident tax year
  • Year 3+: Third and all subsequent years
Target vs Statutory Limit

The target is your conservative planning goal. The statutory limit is the legal maximum. The buffer triggers early warnings when you approach your target.
