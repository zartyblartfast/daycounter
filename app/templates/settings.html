Configuration Guide
Departure Date

The date you physically left the UK. This is used for reference only.

First Full Non-Resident Tax Year

The first complete UK tax year (6 Apr – 5 Apr) after your departure. This determines which profile applies to each subsequent year.

Profile Mapping
  • Year 1: First full non-resident tax year (strictest limits)
  • Year 2: Second year (relaxed limits)
  • Year 3+: Third and all subsequent years
Target vs Statutory Limit

The target is your planning goal (conservative). The statutory limit is the legal maximum. The buffer provides early warning when approaching the target.

These settings are for planning purposes only. Consult a qualified tax adviser.
